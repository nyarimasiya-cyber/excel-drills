Freight Dashboard Project

Build a dynamic management dashboard in Excel from raw TMS exports. There are no worked steps, hints or model answers. You have the brief, the data and the business rules. The checkpoints only tell you whether your figure is right.

Tools Microsoft 365 Excel, Power Query, PivotTables or Power PivotData 7 monthly exports, about 500 jobs and 3,000 charge linesTime about one working day

The brief

WORK ORDER · COMMERCIAL ANALYTICSSouthern Cross Forwarding (fictional)

Southern Cross Forwarding moves sea, air and road cargo for South African importers and exporters through branches in Johannesburg, Cape Town and Durban. Every job can earn money at three stages of the move, and the commercial manager wants to see where the gross profit is made.

OS · Origin ServicesCollection, export clearance, origin handling and documents
FreightThe main carriage by sea, air or road, plus surcharges
DS · Destination ServicesTerminal handling, port dues, import clearance and delivery

Today the branches send spreadsheets by email and nobody trusts the totals. Your job is to turn the monthly TMS exports into one dashboard that management can filter themselves, and that updates when next month's export is dropped into the folder.

Business rules

These define every figure. The checkpoints are calculated with exactly these rules.

  1. A job belongs to the month of its Booking Date.
  2. Cancelled jobs and all their charge lines are excluded from every figure.
  3. Revenue is Sell, cost is Cost, GP is Sell − Cost and GP margin is GP ÷ Revenue.
  4. All reporting is in rand. USD and EUR amounts convert at the FX rate for the job's booking month. ZAR amounts are already in rand.
  5. A charge's group (OS, Freight or DS) is taken from the Charge Codes table.
  6. A job is an Export when its Origin Country is South Africa and an Import otherwise.
  7. TEU: each 20-foot container is 1 TEU and each 40-foot container (40GP or 40HC) is 2 TEU. Sea LCL, Air and Road jobs carry 0 TEU.
  8. On time means a Delivered job whose ATA is on or before its ETA. On-time % is calculated over Delivered jobs only.
  9. Each Line ID is one charge and counts once.
  10. A client is the client named in the Clients table, whatever capitals or spacing the export used. The same applies to Mode.
  11. Round only the final figure, never the individual lines.
