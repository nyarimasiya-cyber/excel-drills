# Excel Analyst Drills

Hands-on practice for analyst roles, in two tracks.

- **Excel** (`index.html`): training notes for 10 topics plus 52 exercises covering core and date formulas, dynamic arrays, lookups, tables and names, LET and LAMBDA, Power Query, DAX and VBA.
- **Python** (`python/index.html`): 13 modules and 77 drills, from variables, strings and loops through NumPy, pandas and charts to a folder-of-Excel-files reporting pipeline. Each module maps Excel formulas to their Python equivalents. A companion Colab notebook (`python/python-analytics-drills.ipynb`) holds every lesson and exercise, with solutions at the end.
- **Project** (`project/index.html`): Freight Dashboard Project, a no-hints capstone. Build a dynamic Excel dashboard from raw freight forwarding TMS exports (OS, Freight and DS charges, TEU, GP, on-time). It comes with the brief, business rules, a logistics glossary and 16 checkpoints that only say right or wrong. Answers are stored as one-way hashes. Data is in `project/data/`, and the company and clients are fictional.

Live site: https://nyarimasiya-cyber.github.io/excel-drills/ (Python track: https://nyarimasiya-cyber.github.io/excel-drills/python/)

Everything runs in the browser; no server or sign-in is needed. Progress is saved in each person's own browser.
